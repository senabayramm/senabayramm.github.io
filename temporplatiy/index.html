Temporal Rules & Accessibility

  • Mouse / Touch: Move or click across the canvas to leave temporal traces that age and fade over time.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Use Arrow Keys to move the cursor, and press Space / Enter to imprint memory.
  • Pause / Resume: Click Pause or press P to freeze time.
  • Reset: Click Reset or press R to clear the temporal archive.
