The system’s state must be understandable. Design the feedback loop, not the outcome.

Controls & Feedback Rules

  • Mouse / Touch: Click top half to apply heat, bottom half to cool down.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Use Arrow Up / Right to heat, Arrow Down / Left to cool.
  • Pause / Resume: Click Pause or press P.
  • Reset: Press R or click Reset to return to thermal equilibrium.
